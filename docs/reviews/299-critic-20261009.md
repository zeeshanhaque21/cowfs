## Determinism and fail-closed asserts
The timeline length varies with a per-process HashMap seed (builder saw 355 to 412 ops).
The fail-closed asserts are tolerant of that:
- `ops.len() > 200` has a wide margin against 280 to 410.
- `images > 1000` is far below roughly 3600.
- `gc_unlinked > 0`, `with_intent > 0` and `old_tree > 0 && new_tree > 0` depend on scripted structure (swap rename and intent unlink are fixed ops), not on HashMap order.
- The negative control needs a `MissingLiveBlock` among about half of roughly 1800 images; that cannot plausibly miss.
- The root-op count assert (two renames) is scripted.
I see no flake risk in the asserts themselves.
Every ack index comes from markers in the same log, so ack-to-cut pairing is internally consistent whatever the order.
